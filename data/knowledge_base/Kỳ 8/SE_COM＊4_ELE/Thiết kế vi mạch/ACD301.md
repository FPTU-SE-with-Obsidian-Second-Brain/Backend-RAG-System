## 2. Mô tả môn học (Description)
1. This course introduces essential concepts and methodologies for CMOS analog integrated circuit design, geared toward electronics engineering students with backgrounds in mathematics, physics, circuit theory, electronics, and signal processing. Emphasizing the integration of analytical and simulation approaches, the course utilizes SPICE simulations to illustrate and verify theoretical concepts, providing a practical approach to accurate circuit design.
2. Key topics include MOS transistor modeling, basic gain stages, multi-stage amplifiers, and feedback theory, all foundational to understanding and designing CMOS circuits. The course also covers two-stage operational amplifiers and biasing circuits, with stability and feedback considerations for practical applications. It concludes with a summary of the essential equations and principles, offering students a comprehensive reference for CMOS analog design.

## 5. Chuẩn đầu ra (Course Learning Outcomes)
- **CLO1:** Analyze CMOS analog circuits using mathematical models and simulations.
- **CLO2:** Apply MOS transistor models in circuit calculations and simulations.
- **CLO3:** Design and evaluate basic gain stages and small-signal models.
- **CLO4:** Construct and understand multi-stage amplifiers.
- **CLO5:** Apply feedback theory to improve amplifier performance and stability.
- **CLO6:** Design and analyze two-stage opamps with stability considerations.
- **CLO7:** Design biasing circuits for amplifier operation.
- **CLO8:** Choose an engineering approach to solving problems, starting from the acquired knowledge essential for the design of electronic circuits.
- **CLO9:** Demonstrate professional ethics, social responsibility, and environmental impacts into electronic component selection, design, and use throughout the entire product life cycle.
