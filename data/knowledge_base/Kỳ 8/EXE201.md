# Experiential Entrepreneurship 2 - Trải nghiệm khởi nghiệp 2

## 1. Thông tin chung
- **Mã môn học (Subject Code):** EXE201
- **Tên tiếng Anh:** Experiential Entrepreneurship 2
- **Số tín chỉ (NoCredit):** 3
- **Môn tiên quyết (Pre-Requisite):** [[EXE101]]
- **Phương pháp dạy:** Online, coaching, project-based learning

## 2. Mô tả môn học (Description)
1. In Experiental Entrepreneurship 2, groups of students will develop product/service for their start-up ideas prepared from Experiental Entrepreneurship 1 and deploy sales and find real customers for their group's products/services.
2. There is no mandatory content for this course, just a list of suggested lectures/talks that students can view to get useful tips and experiences for project realization.
3. In addition, there will be seminars and workshops with related topics planned and implemented each semester according to actual needs and implementation situation.

## 3. Công cụ & Yêu cầu (Tools & Tasks)
### Yêu cầu với sinh viên:
- Students must attend at least 80% of Instructor's Coaching sections, Seminars and Mentor's Mentoring.
- Students are responsible for doing all exercises and assignments given by the instructor in class or at home and submitting them on time.
- Students must meet all milestones' requirements.
- Promplty access to the learning material platform https://flm.fpt.edu.vn/ for up to date course information.

### Công cụ cần thiết:
