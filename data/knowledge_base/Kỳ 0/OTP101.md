## 2. Mô tả môn học (Description)
1. Orientation and general training program includes 4 modules :
2. * Module 1: Orientation
3. Main activities of this module are:
4. - Organizing the opening ceremony for students.
5. - Organizing health check and making students' cards.
6. - Arranging classes for students and organizing class meeting.
7. - Introducing to students about FPT corporation, FPT university, functional departments, training regulations and how to use information systems to support students' learning.
8. - Sharing study skills for university students.
9. - Sharing about topics related to community activities. ( For example: activities towards sustainable development, volunteering activities...)
10. * Module 2: Military training the program prescribed by the Ministry of Education and Training.
11. Implementing the program prescribed by the Ministry of Education and Training.
12. * Module 3: Experience program
13. Main activities of this module are:
14. - Organizing research and review memoirs.
15. - Organizing seminars
16. - Organizing experiential activities for students (Towards sustainable development and volunteering for the community)
17. - Organizing Skill-building extracurricular programs (Training on First Aid and Emergency Response Skills; ...)
18. * Module 4: Vovinam
19. Follow the outline VOV
20. 1
21. 1
22. Objectives of orientation and training program are:
1) Instruct students to complete procedures before a new semester.
2) Provide students with knowledge about FPT corporation, FPT university and functional departments which support students during the period of study at the university.
3) Introduce to students about Curriculum, FU training model and regulations as well as how to use information systems to enable students to adapt new learning environment.
4) Educate students the fundamentals of military and national security, build and enrich patriotism, national pride through history lessons, seminars, documentaries, field trips to military bases and memoirs about two prolonged resistance wars of Viet Nam.
5) Train the willpower and improve physical strengths, fitness and sense of responsibilities through physical education lessons and combat practice in the field.
6) Train team spirit, disciplines, shape good attitude and behaviors towards friends, teachers and educational environment.
7) Enhance student experiences with extra-curricular activities. Strengthen the sense of community through community and volunteering activities and the ones towards the sustainable development.

## 5. Chuẩn đầu ra (Course Learning Outcomes)
- **CLO1:** Know general information about FPT university and the roles of functional department supporting students during the studying period.
- **CLO2:** Define training models and regulations of FPT university, know the curriculum that the students will study and information systems to support students' learning. Understand study skills for university students.
- **CLO3:** Indicate the fundermentals of military and national security and practice the basic skills in military training and Vovinam. Enhance fitness, sense of patriotism and national pride as well as the awareness of constructing and protecting the nation.
- **CLO4:** Develop soft skills such as team spirit, disciplines and positive attitude in different environment.
- **CLO5:** Experience plentiful activities in order to develop personalities, life skills and sense of community.
