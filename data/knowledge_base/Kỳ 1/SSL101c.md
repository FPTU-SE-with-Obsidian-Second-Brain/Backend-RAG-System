# Academic Skills for University Success_Kỹ năng học tập ở Đại học

## 1. Thông tin chung
- **Mã môn học (Subject Code):** SSL101c
- **Số tín chỉ (NoCredit):** 3
- **Môn tiên quyết (Pre-Requisite):** None
- **Phương pháp dạy:** Online

## 2. Mô tả môn học (Description)
1. Upon finishing the course, students can:
2. 1) Knowledge: Understand
3. - Method to develop your Information & Digital Literacy Skills
4. - Method to develop your Problem Solving and Creativity Skills
5. - How to develop your Critical Thinking Skills
6. - How to develop your Communication Skills
7. 2) Able to (ABET)
8. - Access, search, filter, manage and organize Information by using a variety of digital tools, from wide variety of source for use in academic study.
9. - Critically evaluate the reliability of sources and use digital tools for referencing in order to avoid plagiarism.
10. - Demonstrate awareness of ethical issues related to academic integrity surrounding the access and use of information
11. - Develop a toolkit to be able to identify real problems and goals within ill-defined problem & Recognize and apply analytical & creative problem solving technique
12. - Use a variety of thinking tools to improve critical thinking
13. - Identify types of argument, and bias within arguments
14. - Demonstrate, negotiate, and further understanding through spoken, written, visual, and conversational modes
15. - Effectively formulate arguments and communicate research findings through the process of researching, composing, and editing
16. 3) Others: (ABET)
17. - Improve study skills (academic reading, information searching, ...)

## 5. Chuẩn đầu ra (Course Learning Outcomes)
- **CLO1:** Understand and demonstrate how to develop Information & Digital Literacy Skills to help you achieve success in university studies
- **CLO2:** Develop Problem Solving and Creativity Skills
- **CLO3:** Develop Critical Thinking Skills
- **CLO4:** Develop Communication Skills
- **CLO5:** Apply all knowledge of skills learned in the courses to build a Capstone Project
