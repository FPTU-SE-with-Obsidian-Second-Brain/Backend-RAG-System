# Internet of Things_Internet vạn vật

## 1. Thông tin chung
- **Mã môn học (Subject Code):** IOT102
- **Tên tiếng Anh:** Internet of Things
- **Số tín chỉ (NoCredit):** 3
- **Môn tiên quyết (Pre-Requisite):** N/A
- **Phương pháp dạy:** Social Constructivism, Inquiry-Based Learning, In-class lecture, Active Learning, Project-based learning

## 2. Mô tả môn học (Description)
1. This is a 3-credit course. This course has two parts: online and offline.
2. The content includes basic concepts and applications of IoT and practical exercises on learning KIT.
3. Students are taught how to learn online and practice some parts at home. Q&A sessions, Guidance for important issues, and performance assessments will be conducted in the classroom.

## 3. Công cụ & Yêu cầu (Tools & Tasks)

- Student is responsible to do all LABs given by instructor on Syllabus at home.

## 5. Chuẩn đầu ra (Course Learning Outcomes)
- **CLO1:** Understand the concept of IoT and applications.
- **CLO2:** Understand the concept of things in IoT.
- **CLO3:** Understand the Networking in IoT.
- **CLO4:** Understand the Securing in IoT.
- **CLO5:** Select and calculate the electronics components, IoT Programming.
- **CLO6:** Program IN/OUT Digital with Arduino UNO.
- **CLO7:** Program Analog Input/Output with Arduino UNO.
- **CLO8:** Program Sensors/Actuators with Arduino UNO.
- **CLO9:** Program Communication with Arduino UNO.
- **CLO10:** Concept planning, Draw and Install simple IoT circuits
- **CLO11:** Understand the Arduino UNO and Atmega328 microcontroller architecture.
- **CLO12:** Know how to use the Simulation Software.
- **CLO13:** Understand the interrupts on Arduino UNO.
